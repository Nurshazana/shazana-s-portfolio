Case Study · Figma Prototype · March 2025

ePPRNS

Turning business requirements into an interactive prototype for e-Pejabat Pilihan Raya Negeri Sembilan.

Role
Freelance Business Analyst · UI/UX Prototype Designer
Tools & Methods
Figma · Requirements Gathering · Functional Specifications · Interactive User Journeys

01The Problem

Business requirements need to be clear enough for stakeholders to review how a proposed system will work. The project focused on translating user needs into functional specifications and visible user journeys to support that review.

02The Solution

I gathered and validated business requirements, translated user needs into functional specifications, and designed an interactive Figma prototype for stakeholder review and design approval.

Project scope

Business analysis and UI/UX prototyping. The deliverable presented here is an interactive Figma prototype.

03How the Work Fits Together

  1. User needs
  2. Requirements
  3. Functional specifications
  4. Interactive prototype
  5. Stakeholder review

04Key Contributions

Requirements gathering

Gathered and prioritized business requirements to clarify what the proposed system should support.

Functional specifications

Translated user needs into requirements that connect business expectations with system behavior.

Interactive user journeys

Designed linked screens in Figma to make the proposed experience easier to explore.

Stakeholder review

Created a prototype to support feedback and design approval discussions.

05Requirements to Prototype

Business needs↓Functional specifications↓Figma screens & user journeys↓Stakeholder review

06Validation & UAT

The prototype supports review of user journeys against business requirements. Formal UAT execution results and requirement traceability records are not published yet.

Review areas

Requirement coverage, navigation clarity, consistency between screens, and alignment between the proposed workflow and user needs.

07Feedback & Refinement

Interactive screens provide a shared reference for discussing requirements and design choices. A documented feedback example and its resulting design change will be added when supporting records are available.
