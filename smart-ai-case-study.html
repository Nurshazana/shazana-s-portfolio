Case Study · Local Prototype

Smart AI Incident Management

AI-assisted incident investigation and response management, with evidence-based recommendations for human review.

Role
Developer · System Tester · Business Analyst
Technology
Python · FastAPI · Streamlit · PostgreSQL · Ollama (Llama 3.2) · PostgreSQL Full-Text Search

01The Problem

IT support teams investigate service disruptions using information scattered across logs, telemetry, runbooks, and previous incidents. Bringing that evidence together is a necessary step before deciding on a likely cause and a recovery action.

The project addresses both investigation and coordination: tracking ownership, identifying duplicate incidents, and monitoring response deadlines through resolution.

02The Solution

I developed a working local prototype that combines incident management with a locally hosted AI model and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). It analyzes incident symptoms alongside supporting evidence to recommend potential causes and recovery steps for human review.

Current scope

Working local prototype with human-reviewed AI recommendations, notification previews, and sandbox-only automated remediation.

03How It Works

  1. Incident
  2. Evidence retrieval
  3. AI analysis
  4. Root cause recommendation
  5. Human review
  6. Resolution tracking

Retrieved context supports the model's analysis. Recommendations remain subject to human review, and automated remediation is limited to sandbox simulations.

04Key Features

Investigation

AI-assisted diagnosis, RAG evidence retrieval, historical incident context, and recovery recommendations.

Response coordination

Automated assignment, duplicate detection, SLA monitoring, and escalation.

Access and accountability

Role-based access control (RBAC) and audit trails.

Controlled remediation

Human review, notification previews, and sandbox remediation simulations.
